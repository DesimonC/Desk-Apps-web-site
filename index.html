About DesK Apps

Built around real ideas.

DesK Apps is an independent UK app brand developing approachable digital tools for work, home, learning and play. Our aim is simple: make apps that feel useful from the very first tap.

💡Practical by design

Simple, clear tools built to solve everyday problems without unnecessary complexity.

🎮Fun matters too

Games, discovery and learning should feel rewarding, not like hard work.

🤝Shaped by testers

We listen to feedback and improve each app through real-world testing.

Our direction

Our vision & future plans

We're building DesK Apps into a trusted collection of practical, enjoyable digital products — one carefully tested release at a time.

🏢Who we are

DesK Apps is an independent UK app-development brand run by Desmond Kennett. We turn ideas inspired by everyday life into simple tools, games and learning experiences.

🎯Our mission

To make useful technology approachable: clear interfaces, sensible features, enjoyable experiences and fair choices for different users.

📱Our growing portfolio

Our projects span games, family organisation, food planning, staff scheduling, music, nature discovery and more. Each app has its own purpose while sharing the DesK Apps commitment to usability.

🧭Our five-year vision

Over the coming years, we aim to grow from a collection of early projects into an established independent app brand with a regularly improved range of products and an active testing community. Plans will evolve with user needs.

🛠️How we develop

We begin with a real problem or idea, build a usable version, invite feedback, fix issues and refine the experience. We plan releases for web, Android and iOS where each platform is suitable, with availability confirmed per app.

💳Fair ways to access our apps

Different apps may offer free access supported by advertising, optional ad-free upgrades, subscriptions or a one-time purchase. We aim to explain prices and what is included clearly before anyone pays. Poker Hand Compass is planned as a one-time paid, ad-free app.

Our commitment to users

We want our apps to be understandable, accessible and respectful of personal information. Real-world testing, clear feedback routes and ongoing improvements are part of how we work. We will explain app-specific privacy practices and terms before release, and listen when users tell us what could work better.

This page describes our direction, not a promise of specific release dates or features. Financial projections and internal operating plans are not published.

Privacy notice

When you choose to email us through the feedback or tester forms, we receive the personal details you include, such as your name, email address, device and message. We use them to answer enquiries, consider tester requests and improve our apps. We generally rely on our legitimate interests in responding to enquiries or taking steps requested for potential testing arrangements. Forms open your own email application and do not submit information to a website database. Our email provider may store your messages; we review and delete them when no longer necessary. To ask about your data rights, including access, correction or deletion, contact us by email. You may complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.
