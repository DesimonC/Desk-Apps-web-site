Privacy Notice

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This notice explains how DesK Apps handles personal information on its website and across its apps, including Poker Hand Compass and apps currently in testing. Each app only uses the information needed for its features. App data is not combined into a single profile across DesK Apps products.

Controller: Desmond Kennett, operating as DesK Apps. For privacy requests, contact DesK.Apps.DK@gmail.com.

Information collected through this website

If you register to test an app, the form asks for your name, contact email, phone number, device type and model, operating system version, country, the app you want to test, optional notes, consent and registration time. For store testing, it may also ask for the Google Play or Apple account email needed to send an invitation. We do not ask for your account password.

Tester registrations are sent through the website’s Netlify function to a private Google spreadsheet. We use the information to manage testing, select compatible testers and send relevant invitations. We rely on your consent for tester registration and invitations. For service delivery and security, we may rely on our legitimate interests in operating and protecting our website and apps. You can withdraw consent or request access, correction or deletion by emailing us. We keep tester details only while they are needed for the testing programme, then remove them.

If you use a feedback or contact link, your email application opens with a draft. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it. We use the message and your reply address to respond and improve our apps.

Information used by individual apps

Poker Hand Compass

The app does not require an account. Your cards and current practice match are processed in the app and cleared when the page or app session is restarted. Adaptive opponent learning saves summary counts of your betting decisions in local storage on your device; you can clear them using “Reset opponent learning.” The app does not use in-app analytics or advertising in the current build. If you buy the Android app, Google Play handles the purchase and related purchase or device information under Google’s own privacy terms; Poker Hand Compass does not receive your payment card details.

Find It!

To run a game, Find It! processes the display names players choose, a game code, game progress, submitted photos, votes, results, photo reports and private rejoin credentials. Game data and photos are sent to Supabase so participants can play together. Photos are stored privately and shown through temporary links. A private rejoin link contains a player-specific token; keep it private. The server stores a hash of the token and uses it to restore that player and deliver host safety notices. Participants can see the names, submitted photos and game results needed to play and vote. A reported photo is sent to the host for review; the host may remove it, warn the player or block that player from the current game. Reports and notices are part of the game data and are deleted when the host closes the finished game.

When photo screening is enabled, a compressed copy of each submitted photo is sent to Google Cloud Vision SafeSearch before it is stored in Supabase. The screening estimates whether an image may contain adult, suggestive, violent or medical content. Google states that for immediate online requests, images are processed in memory and are not persisted to disk; Google may temporarily log request metadata such as the time and size of a request. The screening can miss unsafe content or flag a suitable image by mistake. If the screening service is unavailable, the photo is not uploaded. See Google Cloud Vision’s data usage information.

When the host closes a finished game, all game photos, including winning photos, winner records and active game data are deleted. This also deletes the game’s reports and rejoin-token hashes.

Competition Hunter

The current guest build does not require an account. It loads competition listings from a Supabase-hosted catalogue. Optional profile details, saved or dismissed competitions, and entry tracking are stored in the browser on that device and are not uploaded as a cloud profile in this build. When you open an organiser’s website, that organiser may collect information under its own privacy notice. Competition Hunter does not submit an entry for you.

Plate-a-Mate

Depending on the features you use, Plate-a-Mate processes profile details and food, meal, calorie, nutrition, hydration, larder, shopping and planning information. Diary entries remain with the individual account. If you connect a family, account and membership details, member names and shared larder stock are processed by the app’s configured database service and shared with family members. Do not add information to the shared larder that you do not want family members to see. This information is for personal planning and is not a medical diagnosis.

Spacestation V3

Friend games use a join code and player names to connect participants. Game progress, turns and other board-game state are processed by the configured Supabase service so the players in that game can see the shared match. Other players in the same game can see the information needed to play together.

Grand Prix Mayhem

When leaderboard features are used, the app may send a player name or chosen display name, score or race time, circuit and result to its configured leaderboard service. Leaderboard entries are visible to other players using that leaderboard.

Spice of Life

The current app has no account, analytics SDK or connected advertising network. Your spice rack, saved blends, favourites and layout preferences are stored locally using app or browser storage. Searches run on your device and are not sent to a search service. The app does not upload these saved selections to DesK Apps. If a future version adds account or cloud-sync features, this notice will be updated before that version is released.

Other apps remain in development. Before an app adds accounts, cloud sync, advertising, analytics, purchases or new data collection, we will update this notice and the app’s store disclosures to explain those features.

Service providers and technical data

Our website is hosted by Netlify. Google services support the tester registration spreadsheet. Some multiplayer or shared features use Supabase. These providers process information on our behalf to host the website, store app data or provide the requested feature. Hosting services may also process technical request data such as IP address, browser type, request time and requested page to deliver and protect their services. Providers may process data in other countries under their own safeguards and terms.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use data from one app to build a profile for another app. Information may be disclosed if required by law or to protect users and our services.

Retention and security

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purposes described here. Retention depends on the feature: temporary game photos are deleted when a Find It! host closes a finished game; local app information remains on your device until you clear it or remove the app; tester records are removed when no longer needed; and some text game results may remain for winner records. We use access controls and service-provider security features appropriate to the service, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You can clear Poker Hand Compass learning data in the app, clear local browser or app data, and choose whether to provide optional details. You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information we hold, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Some information may need to be retained where the law requires it. You may complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

Children

Age suitability varies by app and is shown in the relevant app-store listing. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information through this website. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent us personal information can contact us to request its removal.
